## The collision, stated plainly

The four Psygil decisions all serve a cold-outbound SaaS launch that the adopted plan replaced with a services motion. Specifically:

- **T-0002 / D-0008 / D-0011** (the Hall Render counsel email) scope a Solo-tier BAA review for a self-serve software product. The plan sells a clinician-signed service where the BAA posture runs through the wife's practice, not a self-serve checkout. The employment-agreement attorney review (vault gate 0.5) is the legal gate that is actually on the plan. These may be two different legal questions, and only one is on the live plan.
- **T-0011 / D-0010** (10 cold-sourced discovery interviews) is a SaaS discovery loop. The plan's discovery is warm: the wife's community and three named introductions (vault gate 2.6), contingent on the wife conversation (gate 0.1).
- **T-0012 and the twenty marketing agents** size a cold-email send. The plan deprecated cold email for this list.
- **D-0009** asks whether to re-baseline the Phase 0 calendar. The honest answer may be that this Phase 0 calendar belongs to a plan that is no longer the plan.

## The one question that collapses all four

**Is the Psygil cold-outbound SaaS motion (Wave 1, the Hall Render BAA intake, the 10-interview discovery loop) still a live workstream, or is it superseded by the services motion in the adopted execution plan?**

Three coherent answers, each with a clean consequence:

**A) Superseded. Retire the queue.** The June 11 pivot replaced this motion. Close D-0008, D-0010, D-0011 as superseded, not as failures. Pause T-0004, T-0005, T-0011, T-0012 and the twenty marketing agents. Keep only the items that survive the pivot: the finance ledger and runway (T-0009, real and useful), the outbound-identity cleanup (T-0013), and the Practice-tier and seat-structure questions if the product still ships as a demo. The orchestrator stops aging dead decisions and starts tracking the services motion the vault already runs. Consequence: the daily Psygil stall disappears because the thing stalling is no longer being attempted.

**B) Both live, deliberately.** Psygil-the-product runs a parallel self-serve SaaS track while the vault runs the services track. If so, the queue is valid and the real problem is founder bandwidth split across two go-to-markets. Consequence: the stall is a prioritization problem, not a reconciliation problem, and the decisions stay open with eyes open.

**C) Still deciding.** The pivot is real but the product track is not formally retired. Consequence: park the four decisions under one explicit "Psygil product track: active or retired" decision and stop surfacing them individually until that parent question resolves.

## Recommendation

**A, superseded, with a clean retire rather than a quiet drift.** The evidence is one-directional. The plan adopted June 11 is named the source of truth, it deprecated cold email for this exact list, it moved the first legal gate to the employment agreement, and it made discovery warm. The Psygil queue has not moved in over four weeks because the founder already voted with the pivot and the orchestrator state files never got the memo. Retiring the queue is not giving up on Psygil. It reclasses Psygil as the demo engine the plan already says it is, and it stops five-days-a-week of aging decisions that serve a motion the founder left behind.

If A is right, the one downstream item worth preserving is the legal question itself, reframed: not "send the Hall Render BAA cover," but "does the employment-agreement attorney review (vault gate 0.5) also need to cover the clinical-product BAA posture, or are those separate counsel." That folds the only still-valuable piece of T-0002 into a gate that is already on the live plan.

## What this memo does not do

It does not retire anything. It does not edit MARKETING_PLAN.md (a boundary; founder request required). It does not send the counsel email or close any decision. It stages the reconciliation to the edge and asks for one ruling. The founder's only action is to pick A, B, or C.
